# Foxaira

**Think Fox. Build Smart.**

Foxaira is a technology studio that builds business websites, SaaS platforms, dashboards, AI assistants and workflow automation for businesses in Kuwait, Qatar, Lebanon and the Gulf. Everything supports Arabic and English.

## Our products

- [Hala](https://hala.foxaira.ai/?lang=en): AI customer support on WhatsApp
- [Sites](https://sites.foxaira.ai/?lang=en): AI website builder
- [E-Sites](https://e-sites.foxaira.ai/en): ecommerce platform
- [Menu](https://menu.foxaira.ai/en): QR digital menus for restaurants
- [Links](https://links.foxaira.ai/): link-in-bio pages
